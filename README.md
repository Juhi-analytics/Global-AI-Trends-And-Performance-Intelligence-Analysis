# Global-AI-Trends-And-Performance-Intelligence-Analysis


📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes global Artificial Intelligence (AI) trends by examining country-level data on adoption, investment, infrastructure, talent, and policy. The goal is to uncover patterns that drive AI performance and identify leading and emerging countries in the global AI ecosystem.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow—from data ingestion and cleaning to building an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights.


🎯 Business Problem

With rapid global investment in AI, it is critical to understand:
Which countries are leading in AI development
Whether higher investment translates into better performance
What factors (adoption, infrastructure, talent, policy) drive AI success
Where inefficiencies and growth opportunities exist

📈 Key Insights


Analyzed $26.9T global AI investment and 1.33M startups across 15+ countries (2015–2026) using SQL and Power BI, identifying key trends in AI adoption, performance, and ecosystem efficiency.
Built advanced analytical models using SQL (window functions, aggregations) and Power BI (DAX, dashboards) to rank global AI leaders, consistently identifying China and the USA as dominant AI economies.
Delivered data-driven insights revealing that investment alone does not drive AI performance, with balanced ecosystems outperforming high-investment regions and emerging markets showing the fastest growth trends.
High AI investment does not always guarantee high performance
Enterprise AI adoption strongly correlates with growth
Countries with balanced infrastructure, policy, and talent perform best
Several countries show high readiness but low execution efficiency
AI growth accelerated significantly in recent years
